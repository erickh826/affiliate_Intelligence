# Overview of DALL-E 3

## What is DALL-E 3?

This dall-e 3 review 2026 covers a pivotal moment for OpenAI's image generation technology. DALL·E 3 was OpenAI's third-generation text-to-image model, capable of generating high-resolution images from detailed natural language prompts. The model excelled at understanding nuanced requests and producing photorealistic outputs with strong adherence to prompt specifications. Unlike earlier iterations, DALL·E 3 demonstrated improved handling of hands, text within images, and complex spatial relationships. It competed directly with [Midjourney]({{LINK_MIDJOURNEY}}), [Stable Diffusion]({{LINK_STABLE_DIFFUSION}}), and other leading [AI image generators]({{LINK_AI_IMAGE_GENERATORS}}).

## Key Features and Capabilities

DALL·E 3 offered several distinguishing capabilities:

- **Prompt refinement**: The model automatically rewrote vague prompts into detailed descriptions before generation, reducing iteration cycles
- **Commercial usage rights**: Images generated via API or ChatGPT Plus carried commercial licensing for business applications
- **Resolution**: Generated images at 1024×1024, 1024×1792, and 1792×1024 pixels
- **Speed**: Typical generation times ranged from 10–30 seconds per image

**Pricing structure** (before retirement): API access cost $0.080 per image at standard resolution ($0.040 for preview mode), while ChatGPT Plus subscribers received 50 monthly credits plus overage rates of $0.20 per image.

According to Grand View Research, the global AI image generator market reached $474.2 million in 2025, with projections of $555.1 million in 2026 and $1.853 billion by 2033—reflecting an 18.8% compound annual growth rate through 2033.

## Retirement of DALL-E 3

As of October 16, 2026, DALL·E 3 is no longer available as a current OpenAI API option. OpenAI officially retired the model on May 12, 2026, directing developers toward GPT image models as successors. Similarly, OpenAI retired the official DALL·E GPT within ChatGPT on August 30, 2026, redirecting users to the newer ChatGPT Images feature—notably available on the free tier.

This transition reflects OpenAI's strategic consolidation of image generation capabilities into unified GPT models, prioritizing integration with conversational AI over standalone image APIs.
