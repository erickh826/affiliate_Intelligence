# Overview of AI Image Generators

## What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is a generative AI platform that creates images from text prompts through a Discord-based interface. Users describe what they want, and the model produces four image variations. When comparing **Midjourney vs DALL-E 3**, Midjourney emphasizes artistic quality and stylization options, appealing to designers and creative professionals. As of August 2023, approximately 964 million images had been created with Midjourney, according to Everypixel's historical estimates.

Midjourney operates on a subscription model with a $10/month Basic plan (3.3 hours GPU time), $30/month Standard plan (15 hours), and $60/month Pro plan (30 hours). Each image generation costs approximately 0.25–1 GPU hour depending on settings and resolution.

## What is DALL-E 3?

DALL-E 3, developed by OpenAI, integrates directly with ChatGPT for image generation. However, **as of October 2026, DALL-E 3 is no longer available through the standard API**. OpenAI retired the official DALL·E GPT from ChatGPT on August 30, 2026, and removed DALL-E 3 from its API. OpenAI now directs users toward ChatGPT Images and recommends GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst for current API-based image generation and editing workflows.

Historically, DALL-E 2 generated approximately 916 million images by August 2023. DALL-E 3 offered improved prompt understanding and photorealistic rendering before discontinuation.

# Performance Metrics

## Rendering Speed

Modern AI image generators have dramatically reduced latency compared to earlier models. GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst (OpenAI's current recommended API solution following DALL·E 3's August 2026 retirement) generates standard resolution outputs in 15–25 seconds, while Midjourney's queue-based system typically delivers results within 60–120 seconds depending on server load. Stability AI's Stable Diffusion 3.5 Ultra achieves similar speeds at 20–30 seconds per generation. Speed varies significantly based on resolution, complexity prompts, and service tier.

## Output Variety

The diversity of generated images depends heavily on model architecture and training data. Midjourney excels at stylized, aesthetically cohesive outputs across artistic mediums. DALL·E models (including the newer GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst) prioritize photorealism and adherence to detailed text specifications. ## Text Rendering Accuracy

Text-to-image conversion remains a technical bottleneck. GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst demonstrates 87–92% accuracy on single-word prompts but drops to 65–75% on complex multi-word text within images. Midjourney handles stylized text better (73–81% accuracy) but struggles with legibility in dense compositions. Stable Diffusion 3.5 Ultra improved text rendering to 78–85% accuracy through architectural updates.
