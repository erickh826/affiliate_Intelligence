## What is Brand Voice?

Brand voice is the consistent personality, tone, and linguistic style that defines how your organization communicates across all content channels. A strong brand voice ensures that whether your audience encounters your messaging on social media, email, or blog posts, they immediately recognize it as distinctly *yours*. This consistency builds trust, reinforces brand identity, and creates memorable customer experiences.

This **jasper ai brand voice setup guide** demonstrates how to implement a structured approach to maintaining voice consistency at scale. Rather than manually enforcing tone guidelines across multiple writers and projects, AI-powered brand voice tools automate the process by learning from your existing content examples. Jasper's Brand Voice feature exemplifies this approach, allowing teams to upload representative samples that train the AI to generate content matching your exact style. The setup process is straightforward: open Brand Voice under Jasper IQ, select '+ Add Brand Voice,' and choose whether the voice is private or visible to the workspace. You can upload up to eight examples using pasted text, URLs, or files (.txt, .pdf, .docx formats supported).

Your brand voice is the consistent personality and tone that defines how your organization communicates. ## Accessing the Brand Voice Feature

Jasper's Brand Voice feature lives within Jasper IQ, the platform's core intelligence layer. Here's how to set it up:

1. Log into your Jasper dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Jasper IQ** from the main menu.
3. Locate and click **Brand Voice**.
4. Select the **+ Add Brand Voice** button to create a new voice profile.

Once you've initiated the setup, Jasper will prompt you to provide writing samples that define your voice. The platform accepts up to eight examples, supporting multiple input formats: pasted text directly into the editor, URLs pointing to published content, and file uploads in .txt, .pdf, or .docx formats. This flexibility means you can pull examples from your website, existing blog posts, marketing emails, or brand guidelines documents.

The more representative samples you provide, the better Jasper's AI can capture your distinctive tone—whether that's conversational and friendly, formal and authoritative, or somewhere in between.

## Choosing Between Private and Public Voice

When creating a brand voice, you'll encounter a critical decision: should this voice be **private** or **public** (workspace-visible)?

- **Private Brand Voice**: Only you can access and apply this voice. Use this option for personal writing projects or voice profiles you're still refining.
- **Public Brand Voice**: All workspace members can discover and use this voice in their own copy generation. This is ideal for maintaining consistency across teams and ensuring marketing, sales, and content teams all write in the same brand voice.

For organizations with multiple team members, public voices streamline collaboration and reduce the time spent explaining brand tone in every project brief.
