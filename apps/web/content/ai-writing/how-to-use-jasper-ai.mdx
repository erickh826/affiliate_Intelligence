## Setting Up Your Project

# Setting Up Your Project

Before you generate your first piece of content with Jasper, you need to establish the foundation of your project. This setup phase ensures that all generated content aligns with your brand identity and resonates with your intended audience.

## Defining Your Brand Voice

Your Brand Voice is the personality and tone that distinguishes your content. Jasper lets you create a custom voice by providing up to eight text, file, or URL examples. This might include past blog posts, marketing emails, or product descriptions that exemplify how you want to sound.

Once you've uploaded your examples, Jasper generates a preview of content with and without your Brand Voice applied. This side-by-side comparison lets you refine the voice before saving it to your project. You can set Brand Voice at the Project level (applying it across all Assets) or customize it for individual Assets when you need variation.

## Choosing Your Audience

Defining your audience shapes how Jasper tailors its output. Rather than generating generic content, Jasper recommends including specific audience details in your prompts:

1. Identify your target demographic (age, profession, experience level)
2. List relevant keywords your audience searches for
3. Include your desired call-to-action (CTA)
4. Generate initial content and review the output
5. Adjust your prompt based on results and regenerate

This iterative approach ensures the AI understands not just *who* you're writing for, but *what action* you want them to take.

# Optimizing Your Prompts

Crafting effective prompts is the foundation of generating high-quality output from [AI writing tools]({{LINK_AI_WRITING}}). Jasper's prompt optimization framework helps users structure requests that consistently produce on-brand, audience-targeted content.

## What's the best way to write prompts in Jasper?

Effective prompts are specific and directional. Jasper recommends including three core elements:

- **Intended audience** (e.g., "busy B2B SaaS founders")
- **Target keywords** (e.g., "AI workflow automation," "productivity tools")
- **Clear call to action** (e.g., "Sign up for a free trial," "Download the guide")

**Example prompt structure:**
```
Write a 150-word LinkedIn post about AI email tools for busy professionals 
(audience: marketing managers). Include the keyword "email automation" 
and end with "Try it free for 14 days." Make the tone conversational and 
industry-aware.
```

After generating content, **iterate on your prompt** based on the output. If the tone isn't right, specify adjectives like "witty," "authoritative," or "concise." If coverage is thin, ask for specific benefits or use cases. This feedback loop typically produces stronger results than static prompts.
