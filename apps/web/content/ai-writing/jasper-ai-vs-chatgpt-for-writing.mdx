# Overview of Jasper AI and ChatGPT

## What is Jasper AI?

Jasper AI is a generative AI platform purpose-built for marketing and content teams. When evaluating **Jasper AI vs ChatGPT for writing**, it's important to understand that Jasper specializes in brand-voice consistency and campaign-scale content production. The platform integrates with existing marketing workflows, offering templates for emails, social media, product descriptions, and long-form content. Jasper's AI learns your brand guidelines and maintains voice consistency across outputs—a critical feature for teams managing multiple campaigns simultaneously.

## What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational AI model available as a free tool and through paid subscription tiers. It functions as a general-purpose assistant, excelling at answering questions, brainstorming, coding, and content ideation. ChatGPT lacks native marketing templates and brand-voice preservation, but its versatility makes it suitable for diverse tasks beyond writing.
