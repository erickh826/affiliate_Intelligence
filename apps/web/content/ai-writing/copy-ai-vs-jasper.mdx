# Overview of AI Writing Assistants

## What are AI Writing Assistants?

AI writing assistants are software tools powered by large language models that help creators, marketers, and professionals generate, edit, and optimize written content. When evaluating options like Copy AI vs Jasper, users should understand that both platforms leverage generative AI to automate drafting, brainstorming, and refinement tasks. These tools integrate into workflows to reduce time spent on routine writing while maintaining quality and brand voice. Unlike basic grammar checkers, modern AI writing assistants understand context, tone, and intent—enabling them to produce marketing copy, blog posts, emails, and technical documentation.
