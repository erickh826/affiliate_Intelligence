# Overview of Grammarly AI in 2026

## What is Grammarly AI?

A grammarly ai review 2026 would show that Grammarly has become one of the most widely adopted AI writing assistants globally. The platform uses machine learning to analyze text in real time, identifying grammar, spelling, punctuation, clarity, and tone issues across emails, documents, social media posts, and web forms. Unlike basic spell-checkers, Grammarly's AI engine understands context, writing style, and audience intent—offering personalized suggestions rather than one-size-fits-all corrections.

The global AI writing assistant software market reflects Grammarly's dominance: Fortune Business Insights valued the sector at $2.71 billion in 2025, with forecasts of $3.40 billion in 2026 and $20.78 billion by 2034, representing a projected CAGR of 25.38% through 2034.

## Key Features of Grammarly

Grammarly offers tiered access: a free browser extension for basic checks (grammar, spelling, plagiarism detection limited to 100 checks monthly) and premium plans starting at **$12/month** (annual billing) or $30/month (monthly). Premium unlocks advanced features: tone detection, style improvements, generative AI rewrites, and full plagiarism scanning across the web.

The platform integrates with Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, Gmail, LinkedIn, and 500+ web applications, allowing users to write directly within their preferred tools.

## User Base and Popularity

Grammarly reports that 40 million people and 50,000 organizations use its service daily—company-reported figures reflecting significant market penetration. This user scale has made it the leading [AI Writing Tools]({{LINK_AI_WRITING}}) solution for professionals, students, and teams.
