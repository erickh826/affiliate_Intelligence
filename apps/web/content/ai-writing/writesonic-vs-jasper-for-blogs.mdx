# Overview of Writesonic and Jasper

## What is Writesonic?

**Writesonic vs Jasper for blogs** comes down to workflow preferences and feature priorities. Jasper is an enterprise-grade AI writing assistant built for teams and organizations needing brand consistency and collaboration features. Launched in 2021, Jasper emphasizes long-form content creation, brand voice customization, and multi-user workspaces. It powers content workflows for marketing agencies and large publishing operations with advanced features like content calendars and SEO optimization built-in.
