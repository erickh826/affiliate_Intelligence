# How to Use Grammarly AI: A Comprehensive Tutorial

## Introduction to Grammarly AI

# Introduction to Grammarly AI

## What is Grammarly AI?

Grammarly AI is an intelligent writing assistant that leverages generative AI to help users draft, revise, and refine text across multiple platforms. Learning **how to use Grammarly AI** is straightforward: the tool integrates into your writing workflow, offering real-time suggestions and advanced rewriting capabilities. Whether you're composing emails, reports, or creative content, Grammarly AI adapts to your needs by providing contextual feedback and generating alternative phrasings. Unlike basic grammar checkers, this [AI writing assistant]({{LINK_AI_WRITING}}) understands tone, clarity, and intent, making it valuable for professionals, students, and content creators alike.

## Benefits of Using Grammarly AI

**Enhanced Writing Quality**: Grammarly AI catches grammatical errors while improving readability and tone consistency.

**Time Savings**: Generative AI features accelerate drafting and editing, reducing manual revision cycles.

**Flexible Integration**: Works across Windows, Mac, web browsers, and third-party applications.

**Customizable Assistance**: Accept suggested rewrites or input custom prompts for tailored output.

## Overview of Features

### How to Access Generative AI Revisions

1. Select the text you want to improve
2. Click the pencil icon, lightbulb, or paragraph sidebar
3. Review suggested rewrites or enter a custom prompt
4. Choose "Accept" to apply the generated text

In the Grammarly Editor, the **"Write with generative AI"** tab helps you start outlines or full drafts from scratch.

### AI Chat for Windows and Mac

Grammarly AI Chat accepts open-ended prompts for drafting, feedback, and rewriting tasks. Users can select text, choose "Revise with AI," and apply generated content with one click.

### Pricing & Limits

Grammarly's AI capabilities vary by plan:

- **Free**: 100 generative AI prompts per month
- **Pro**: 2,000 prompts per member per month ($12/month billed annually)

Additionally, Windows and Mac AI Chat supports up to 1,000 chat prompts per day, providing substantial capacity for power users.

## Getting Started with Grammarly AI

# Getting Started with Grammarly AI

## Creating a Grammarly Account

Grammarly's generative AI features are accessible through a straightforward account setup. The platform offers two main tiers: **Free** (with 100 generative AI prompts per month) and **Pro** (with 2,000 prompts per member per month). To begin:

1. Visit Grammarly's website and enter your email address
2. Create a password and verify your email
3. Choose your plan (Free or Pro)
4. Grant browser or application permissions as needed
5. Start writing or uploading documents

The Free tier is ideal for users testing AI-assisted writing, while Pro subscribers benefit from higher prompt limits—approximately 20× more monthly capacity for intensive drafting and revision workflows.

## Navigating the Grammarly Editor

Once logged in, you'll interact with Grammarly's core writing interface. The generative AI tools integrate directly into your workflow:

**Using AI Rewriting:**
- Select any text passage in the editor
- Click the pencil, lightbulb, or paragraph sidebar button that appears
- Review suggested rewrites or enter a custom prompt for specific revisions
- Choose an option or reject all suggestions

**Drafting with AI:**
The "Write with generative AI" tab in the Grammarly Editor helps you start outlines or generate initial drafts. This feature accelerates the blank-page problem by providing structural scaffolding or content seeds you can refine.

**Important Limits:** Free users receive 100 prompts monthly; Pro subscribers get 2,000 prompts. Each rewrite suggestion or custom prompt counts toward your monthly allocation, so plan accordingly for high-volume writing projects.

## Installing Grammarly on Different Devices

Grammarly AI Chat—a dedicated feature on Windows and Mac—expands your capabilities beyond the web editor. Installation steps:

1. Download the Grammarly desktop app from grammarly.com
2. Run the installer and follow on-screen prompts
3. Log in with your account credentials
4. Enable integrations with your preferred applications (Word, Google Docs, email clients, etc.)

On Windows and Mac, AI Chat accepts open-ended prompts for drafting, feedback, and rewriting. Select text in any supported application, choose "Revise with AI," and click "Accept" to integrate generated suggestions. Note that AI Chat operates under a separate limit: 1,000 chat prompts per day.

For mobile users, Grammarly's iOS and Android apps provide core writing assistance and grammar checking, though generative AI features remain most robust on desktop platforms.

## Using Grammarly AI for Text Revision

# Using Grammarly AI for Text Revision

Grammarly's generative AI features allow you to revise, rewrite, and refine your text directly within the editor or through AI Chat. Below are the core workflows for selecting text, applying rewrites, and accepting suggestions.

## Selecting Text for Revision

Start by identifying the text you want to improve. In Grammarly Editor, highlight your selection and look for the AI revision interface. A pencil icon, lightbulb, or paragraph sidebar will appear, signaling that generative AI assistance is available. This works across platforms—whether you're using the web editor, browser extension, or native Windows and Mac applications.

For more advanced workflows, open the **"Write with generative AI"** tab in Grammarly Editor. This feature helps you create outlines, draft new sections, or completely reframe existing passages before you commit them to your document.

## Using the AI Rewrite Feature

Once text is selected, you have two options:

1. **Choose a suggested rewrite** – Grammarly displays multiple AI-generated alternatives tailored to tone, clarity, or brevity.
2. **Enter a custom prompt** – Type specific instructions (e.g., "Make this more conversational" or "Reduce this to one sentence") for fine-tuned results.

On Windows and Mac, Grammarly AI Chat accepts open-ended prompts for drafting, feedback, and rewriting. You can describe what you need, and the AI generates customized text suggestions within the chat interface.

## Accepting AI Suggestions

After reviewing generated text, click **"Accept"** to apply the rewrite to your document. The original text is replaced instantly, though you can undo the change if needed. This workflow repeats for each section you revise.

### Pricing & Limits

Grammarly's generative AI access depends on your plan:

- **Free plan**: 100 generative AI prompts per month
- **Pro plan**: 2,000 prompts per member per month

On Windows and Mac, AI Chat enforces an additional daily limit of 1,000 chat prompts per day, ensuring fair resource allocation during peak usage.

For teams managing multiple writers, the [Grammarly Enterprise]({{LINK_GRAMMARLY_BUSINESS}}) plan scales these limits across your organization while centralizing style guidelines and compliance standards.

## Drafting with Grammarly AI

# Drafting with Grammarly AI

Grammarly's generative AI transforms the drafting process by automating outline creation, draft generation, and content refinement. Whether you're starting from scratch or revising existing work, these tools streamline writing workflows across multiple platforms.

## Starting an Outline with AI

The "Write with generative AI" tab in the Grammarly Editor jumpstarts your creative process. Rather than staring at a blank page, you can prompt Grammarly to generate structured outlines tailored to your topic and tone. This feature is particularly useful for long-form content like blog posts, reports, or academic papers.

To access this feature:

1. Open the Grammarly Editor
2. Click the "Write with generative AI" tab
3. Enter your topic or working title
4. Review the suggested outline structure
5. Refine sections or prompt for additional details

## Generating Drafts Using AI

Once you have an outline, Grammarly can expand it into full drafts. Select any text in your document and click the pencil, lightbulb, or paragraph sidebar that appears. You'll see suggested rewrites or can enter a custom prompt for more control.

On Windows and Mac, the AI Chat feature offers additional flexibility:

```
1. Select text you want to revise
2. Choose "Revise with AI" from the context menu
3. Review the generated alternative
4. Click "Accept" to apply or "Reject" to discard
```

For precise, on-brand content, custom prompts unlock Grammarly's full potential. Instead of accepting default suggestions, you can specify tone, style, audience, and structure—giving AI direction aligned with your goals.

**Prompt example:**
"Rewrite this product description as a conversational email to small business owners, emphasizing ROI and ease of setup."

# Grammarly AI vs. Other Writing Tools

## Comparison with QuillBot

Grammarly and QuillBot both offer AI-powered rewriting, but their approaches differ significantly. QuillBot specializes in paraphrasing and tone adjustment, with a focus on synonym replacement and structural variation. Grammarly's generative AI, by contrast, handles broader tasks—from grammar correction to stylistic enhancement to content generation.

**Pricing and Usage Limits:**
- **Grammarly Free**: 100 generative AI prompts per month
- **Grammarly Pro**: 2,000 AI prompts per member per month ($30 per member/month billed monthly)
- **QuillBot Premium**: $8.33/month billed annually ($99.95/year), or $19.95 billed monthly

Grammarly's AI Chat on Windows and Mac enforces a separate limit of 1,000 chat prompts per day, allowing power users more flexibility for intensive sessions.

## Comparison with ProWritingAid

ProWritingAid excels at detailed style analysis and in-depth reports on readability, repetition, and pacing. Grammarly AI offers faster, more intuitive rewriting through generative capabilities.

**Key workflow difference:**

1. In **Grammarly**, select your text
2. Click the pencil, lightbulb, or paragraph sidebar
3. Choose a suggested rewrite or enter a custom prompt
4. Click "Accept" to apply changes

ProWritingAid requires more manual engagement with its report interface, though it provides richer diagnostic data.

For the [Grammarly Editor]({{LINK_GRAMMARLY_EDITOR}}), the "Write with generative AI" tab helps outline or draft from scratch—a feature less developed in ProWritingAid's free tier.

## Choosing the Right Tool for Your Needs

**Choose Grammarly if:**
- You want speed and ease of use with AI-assisted rewriting
- You need real-time corrections across multiple platforms
