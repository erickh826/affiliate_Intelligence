## Common Complaints and Limitations

# Common Complaints and Limitations

AI writing tools have gained mainstream adoption—21% of U.S. workers reported using AI for at least some of their work in a September 2025 Pew Research Center survey, up from 16% a year earlier. Within ChatGPT specifically, writing accounts for roughly 40% of work-related messages as of mid-2025. Yet despite this rapid growth, users frequently encounter three persistent problems.

## Generic or Repetitive Output

Many AI writing tools produce formulaic content that lacks originality. Models trained on large datasets tend to default to common phrases and sentence structures, resulting in text that sounds "like AI." Users report that outputs require substantial editing to feel authentic, particularly for brand voice or creative projects. This limitation becomes more apparent when generating similar content types repeatedly—email sequences, product descriptions, or social media captions often share syntactic patterns even when prompts differ significantly.

## Inaccuracies and Fact-Checking

AI writing tools frequently hallucinate facts, misrepresent statistics, or cite nonexistent sources. Without real-time internet access or robust fact-checking mechanisms, tools cannot distinguish between accurate and fabricated information. This creates a critical bottleneck: users must verify every claim independently, negating time-saving benefits for research-heavy content.
