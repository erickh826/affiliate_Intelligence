# Overview of Jasper AI

## What is Jasper AI?

This jasper ai review 2026 covers a generative AI platform built specifically for marketing and content teams. Jasper uses large language models to automate writing tasks—from blog posts and social-media copy to email campaigns and product descriptions. Unlike general-purpose AI chatbots, Jasper bundles brand voice preservation, SEO optimization, and team collaboration features into a single workspace designed for marketers and agencies.

The platform launched in 2021 and has positioned itself as an enterprise-grade alternative to [AI writing tools]({{LINK_AI_WRITING}}) targeting businesses that need compliance and data-privacy controls. According to HubSpot's 2025 survey of more than 1,000 marketing and advertising professionals worldwide, 66% of marketers said they use AI in their roles, and 52% reported using generative AI for text-based content creation—the exact use case Jasper serves. However, adoption faces headwinds: 42% of surveyed marketers said data-privacy concerns prevented their team from adopting new AI tools in the preceding year.

## Key Features of Jasper AI

Jasper offers template-driven content creation, real-time brand voice training, plagiarism detection, and native integrations with tools like Slack and HubSpot. The platform includes a Campaigns feature for managing multi-channel content workflows. Jasper also provides API access for custom integrations, with rate limits of 60 requests per minute on the free tier and higher limits available on paid plans.

Pricing starts at $39/month for individual users (annual billing), with team plans beginning at $125/month. Enterprise pricing requires custom quotes.
