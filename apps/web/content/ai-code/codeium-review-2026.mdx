### Introduction to Codeium and Its Evolution

In the realm of AI-powered development tools, the journey of Codeium has been both dynamic and transformative. #### The Birth of Codeium

Codeium was initially launched as a cutting-edge AI tool designed to assist developers with code completion and debugging. Its primary goal was to streamline coding workflows, offering a robust set of features that quickly gained popularity among developers. With its intuitive interface and powerful AI capabilities, Codeium set a new standard for AI-assisted coding tools.

#### Transition to Windsurf

On April 4, 2025, Codeium underwent a significant transformation, officially rebranding to Windsurf. This change was not just in name but also in functionality, as the original Codeium extensions were renamed to Windsurf Plugins. The transition marked a new chapter for the tool, aiming to expand its reach and capabilities ([source](https://devin.ai/blog/windsurf-rebrand-announcement)). The rebranding was part of a strategic move to align the product more closely with emerging market trends and user demands.

#### Rebranding to Devin Desktop

The evolution continued when Windsurf became Devin Desktop on June 2, 2026. According to its official FAQ, this rebranding did not alter existing editor features, extensions, settings, or workflows, ensuring a seamless transition for current users ([source](https://docs.devin.ai/desktop/devin-desktop-faq.md)). Importantly, the rebranding did not affect existing subscription pricing, maintaining stability for its user base.

As of October 29, 2026, Devin offers a tiered pricing model: a Free plan, Pro at $20/month, and Max at $200/month. For teams, Devin provides a plan at $40/month per full seat, with a minimum of $80/month, meaning two full seats cost $80/month before additional usage ([source](https://docs.devin.ai/admin/billing/self-serve)). However, it's crucial to note that Flex seats do not include Devin Desktop.

In summary, Codeium's evolution into Devin Desktop reflects a strategic adaptation to the changing landscape of AI development tools. Its consistent pricing and feature retention ensure that it remains a reliable choice for developers seeking advanced coding assistance.
