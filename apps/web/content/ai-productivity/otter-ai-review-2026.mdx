In this otter ai review 2026, we explore how Otter AI stands out as a leading transcription service that utilizes artificial intelligence to convert spoken language into written text. #### Key Features of Otter AI

Otter AI offers a range of features designed to enhance productivity and streamline workflows. Key features include:

- **Real-Time Transcription**: Otter AI provides live transcription services, allowing users to get instant text versions of their spoken words.
- **Speaker Identification**: The tool can distinguish between different speakers, which is crucial for meetings involving multiple participants.
- **Integration Capabilities**: Otter AI integrates seamlessly with popular platforms like Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, enhancing its utility in virtual meetings.
- **Search and Highlight**: Users can search through transcripts and highlight important sections for easy reference later.

## Pricing Plans for Otter AI in 2026

### Otter Basic Plan

The Otter Basic Plan is designed for individuals who need essential transcription services without a financial commitment. As of 2026, this plan remains free, allowing users to transcribe up to 600 minutes per month. However, each transcription is capped at 30 minutes. This plan is perfect for casual users who need occasional transcription support without the need for advanced features or extensive usage.

### Otter Pro Plan

For users requiring more robust features, the Otter Pro Plan is available at $16.99 per month, billed annually. This plan offers 6,000 minutes of transcription per month, with no time limits on individual recordings. Additionally, it includes advanced features such as custom vocabulary, audio imports, and the ability to export transcriptions in various formats. The Pro Plan is ideal for professionals and businesses that rely heavily on accurate and comprehensive transcription services.
