# Overview of Notion AI and ChatGPT

## What is Notion AI?

When comparing **notion ai vs chatgpt**, it's essential to understand that Notion AI is a workspace-native assistant built directly into Notion's productivity platform, while ChatGPT operates as a standalone conversational interface. Notion AI integrates seamlessly with your databases, documents, and workflows, enabling you to generate summaries, brainstorm ideas, and draft content without leaving your workspace. Unlike ChatGPT's web-based chat model, Notion AI understands the context of your existing pages and can transform your notes into polished outputs.

Notion AI is available as an add-on feature bundled with Business and Enterprise plans. Free and Plus tier users receive limited AI trial credits, but ongoing access requires an upgrade. As of October 2026, Notion Business costs $20 per member monthly with annual billing ($24 monthly), while Enterprise pricing is custom-quoted based on organizational needs.
