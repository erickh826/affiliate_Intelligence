### Asana: AI Teammates and Features

Asana is renowned for its AI teammates, which help automate routine tasks and provide intelligent insights. As of November 3, 2026, Asana's Starter plan costs $10.99 per user per month when billed annually, or $13.49 if billed monthly. This plan includes five AI Teammate requests per user per month, capped at 50 per account, and 50,000 AI Studio credits per billing account per month. Should you need more, additional AI requests are priced at $0.50 prepaid or $0.60 on a pay-as-you-go basis. This pricing structure allows teams to scale their AI usage according to their needs, making it a flexible option for growing businesses.

### ClickUp: AI Agents and Artifacts

ClickUp stands out for its AI agents and artifacts, which are designed to optimize task management and collaboration. The platform offers two main pricing tiers: Core and Business. The Core plan is priced at $9 per user per month when billed annually, with a regular price of $12. It includes 1,000 pooled AI credits per user per month and a Brain² trial. The Business plan, at $17 per user per month (regularly $25), offers 1,500 pooled credits per user per month and unlimited standard-model use, subject to fair use policies. These AI capabilities are ideal for teams looking to enhance their project management processes with intelligent automation and insights.

### Wrike: Risk Prediction and AI Capabilities

Wrike is highlighted for its robust risk prediction and AI capabilities, which help project managers anticipate potential issues and mitigate risks effectively.
