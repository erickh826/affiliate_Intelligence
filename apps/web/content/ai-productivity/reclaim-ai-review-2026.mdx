Reclaim AI is a smart scheduling assistant that helps users manage their time more effectively by integrating with their existing calendars. Unlike traditional scheduling tools, Reclaim AI adapts to your changing priorities and workload, offering a dynamic approach to time management.

### Key Features of Reclaim AI 2.0

Reclaim AI 2.0 introduces several enhancements over its predecessor. One significant change is that it no longer automatically creates a dedicated calendar event for each task. Instead, it suggests tasks during available Focus Time, allowing for a more fluid and less cluttered calendar experience. This update is available to users who signed up after May 2026.
