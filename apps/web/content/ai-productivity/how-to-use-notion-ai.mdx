## Getting Started with Notion AI

# Getting Started with Notion AI

Notion AI transforms how teams collaborate and manage information within Notion workspaces. Learning how to use Notion AI unlocks powerful automation capabilities—from generating content to organizing databases. This guide walks you through accessing the tool, configuring your workspace, and mastering the interface so you can immediately boost productivity.

## Accessing Notion AI

Notion AI is integrated directly into your workspace. You'll find it via a circular face icon in the top navigation or by selecting **New chat** in the sidebar. Once activated, the Notion Agent becomes your workspace assistant, capable of creating pages or databases, searching information, and updating content in real time.

Notion AI is available as an add-on for $10 per member per month (billed annually) or $15 monthly. Teams can also access it through the Business Trial plan, which includes a 30-day evaluation period.

## Setting Up Your Workspace

To maximize Notion AI's potential, prepare your workspace:

1. **Enable AI features** in workspace settings under "AI & automation"
2. **Organize your database structure** so the Agent can access relevant information
3. **Define template properties** for consistent data tagging and categorization
4. **Create dedicated AI chat spaces** for brainstorming or research tasks

For **AI Meeting Notes**, type `/meet` on any Notion page, then start and stop your recording session. The AI automatically transcribes and summarizes discussions. **AI Autofill** processes database entries—use it to summarize content, apply tags, or categorize records without manual input.

## Understanding the Interface

Notion recommends crafting prompts with:

- **Clear goals**: "Generate a Q3 marketing calendar"
- **Relevant context**: "Based on our past campaign performance data"
- **Audience & tone**: "For executive stakeholders, professional tone"
- **Format & length**: "Bullet points, maximum 150 words"

Follow-up questions refine outputs further. Ask the Agent to adjust tone, add examples, or expand specific sections.

The chat interface displays your conversation history, making it easy to iterate on results. You can save AI-generated pages directly to your workspace or copy content to clipboard for editing elsewhere.

## Leveraging AI Meeting Notes

# Leveraging AI Meeting Notes

Recording and documenting meetings is one of the most time-consuming yet critical tasks for modern teams. AI-powered meeting note generation eliminates manual transcription while preserving accuracy and context. Notion AI offers a streamlined approach to capture, organize, and act on meeting insights directly within your workspace.

## How to Start an AI Meeting Session

Notion AI is accessible from the circular face icon in your workspace or by selecting **New chat** in the sidebar. Once enabled, you can initiate meeting capture by typing the `/meet` command on any Notion page.

**Steps to begin an AI meeting session:**

1. Open or create a Notion page where you want to log meeting notes.
2. Type `/meet` and press Enter to activate the meeting capture tool.
3. Grant microphone permissions when prompted.
4. Start your meeting or conversation; Notion AI begins recording automatically.
5. Press **Stop** when the meeting concludes.
6. Review and edit the auto-generated transcript before saving.

The AI then processes the raw transcript into structured notes. You can ask Notion Agent to create follow-up pages, databases, or action items based on the meeting content—eliminating the need for manual data entry across your workspace.

## Transcribing Meetings: Best Practices

Notion AI Meeting Notes work best when you provide context upfront. State a clear goal for the meeting and specify relevant details: audience, desired tone, format, and expected output length. For example, instead of "summarize this meeting," try "create a bulleted action-item list with assigned owners and due dates from this product planning meeting."

After transcription, refine the output by asking follow-up questions. You might request that AI tag entries by priority, categorize discussion points by department, or extract specific decisions. AI Autofill can also summarize, tag, or categorize database entries, enabling you to organize meeting notes alongside existing workspace content.

## Respecting Participant Privacy

Ensure all meeting participants consent to AI recording and transcription. Notion's privacy controls allow you to restrict who can view meeting notes within your workspace. Store sensitive transcripts in gated databases with role-based access permissions. Regularly audit meeting archives and delete recordings once they're no longer needed for compliance or record-keeping purposes.

## Effective Prompting Techniques

# Effective Prompting Techniques

Getting the most from Notion AI requires deliberate prompt construction. The difference between a generic response and exactly what you need often comes down to how clearly you frame your request. Here are three proven techniques to elevate your prompting game.

## Crafting Clear Goals

Your prompt's foundation is a specific, actionable goal. Vague requests produce vague results. Instead of "write something about our Q3 strategy," try:

> "Create a one-page executive summary of our Q3 strategy focused on revenue targets, with sections for: Goals, Key Initiatives, and Risk Mitigation."

Notion AI responds to clarity. When you specify *what* you want (summary vs. brainstorm vs. analysis), the AI allocates its capabilities toward that outcome. Access Notion AI from the circular face icon in your workspace or by selecting **New chat** in the sidebar. Once open, you can ask Notion Agent to create pages or databases, search information, and update your workspace content.

## Specifying Context and Audience

Context transforms output quality. Notion recommends including:
- **Audience**: Who reads this? (e.g., C-suite executives, engineering team, clients)
- **Tone**: Formal, casual, technical, conversational?
- **Format**: Bullet points, prose, table, checklist?
- **Length**: Word count or section count

Example prompt:
> "Summarize last month's customer feedback in 200 words, formatted as three bullet points, for a board presentation. Tone: professional but accessible."

## Asking Follow-Up Questions

Refinement happens iteratively. After Notion AI generates initial output, ask clarifying questions:

**Steps to refine AI Meeting Notes:**
1. Type `/meet` on a Notion page to enable AI Meeting Notes
2. Start your meeting; Notion records and transcribes automatically
3. Stop the session when complete
4. Review the generated notes
5. Ask follow-up questions: "Condense this to action items only" or "Highlight risks mentioned"

Similarly, **AI Autofill** (available in Notion databases) summarizes, tags, or categorizes entries. Ask it to recategorize if initial results miss your intent.

[Notion AI pricing]({{LINK_NOTION_PRICING}}) starts at **$10/month per user** when added to any Notion plan, with usage limits of 20 AI actions per month on the free tier. Paid tiers unlock 100+ actions monthly, supporting heavier prompt iteration.

The pattern is simple: state a goal → provide context → refine via follow-up. This workflow, combined with [AI Meeting Tools]({{LINK_AI_MEETING}}) and [AI Writing Tools]({{LINK_AI_WRITING}}) integration, transforms Notion from a database into an intelligent workspace assistant.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

# Troubleshooting Common Issues

## Resolving Access Problems

If you can't access Notion AI, verify that your workspace has an active AI add-on subscription. Notion AI costs $8/month per user (as of 2024) and must be enabled by a workspace admin. Check access via the circular face icon in your workspace header or by selecting **New chat** in the sidebar.

**Steps to restore access:**

1. Navigate to workspace Settings > Members & Guests
2. Confirm your user role has AI permissions enabled
3. Verify your subscription is active under Settings > Billing
4. Refresh your browser (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows)
5. Clear cached data if the issue persists

If you're using AI Meeting Notes, ensure you've typed `/meet` on a Notion page and properly started the recording session. The feature requires an active microphone and stable internet connection. Stop the session before generating notes.

## Fixing AI Response Errors

AI responses may be incomplete or inaccurate if your prompts lack clarity. Notion recommends structuring requests with:

- **Clear goal**: "Summarize this document for executives"
- **Relevant context**: Include background information or existing data
- **Audience & tone**: Specify technical level and voice
- **Format & length**: Define output structure (bullet points, paragraph, table)

For example, a weak prompt is "Fix this text." A strong prompt is: "Rewrite this product description for a casual tone, 50 words max, aimed at Gen Z social media users."

AI Autofill errors often stem from incomplete database schemas. Verify your database properties are clearly labeled and contain sample data before running summarization, tagging, or categorization tasks. If responses seem off-topic, ask follow-up questions to refine the AI's understanding—Notion Agent can search information and update workspace content based on clarified instructions.

**Common error resolutions:**

- Regenerate responses by clicking the refresh icon
- Simplify complex multi-step requests into sequential prompts
- Add specific examples to your prompt for better accuracy
- Ensure database fields are consistently formatted

## Contacting Support for Help

If troubleshooting doesn't resolve your issue, access Notion's help center at [help.notion.com](https://www.notion.com/help). For account-specific problems, click the **Help** icon (?) in your workspace and select **Send feedback** or **Contact support**.

Document your issue with screenshots, the exact prompt you used, and your workspace ID (found in Settings > Workspace). Support typically responds within 24–48 hours for paid plans. For urgent issues, consider checking the [Notion Community]({{LINK_NOTION_COMMUNITY}}) where other users may have encountered similar problems.
