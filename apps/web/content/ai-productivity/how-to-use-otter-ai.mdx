## Integrating Otter AI with Online Meetings

### Integrating Otter AI with Online Meetings

Otter AI offers a seamless way to transcribe your online meetings, enhancing productivity by providing accurate meeting notes. This guide will walk you through connecting your calendars, joining meetings, and managing settings within Otter AI.

#### Connecting Google and Microsoft Calendars

To make the most of Otter AI, start by integrating your Google or Microsoft calendar. This integration allows Otter Notetaker to automatically join and transcribe your scheduled meetings. Follow these steps to connect your calendar:

1. Sign in to your Otter AI account.
2. Navigate to the "Settings" menu.
3. Select "Integrations" and choose either "Google Calendar" or "Microsoft Calendar."
4. Follow the prompts to authorize Otter AI to access your calendar.

By connecting your calendar, you ensure that Otter AI can automatically identify and join your meetings, providing real-time transcription services.

#### Joining Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams

Once your calendar is linked, you can specify which meetings Otter Notetaker should join. This feature supports popular platforms like Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Here's how to set it up:

1. In the Otter AI dashboard, go to "Meetings."
2. Select "Manage Meetings" to view your upcoming schedule.
3. Choose which meetings you want Otter Notetaker to join by toggling the corresponding option.

Additionally, Otter AI allows you to import existing audio or video files for transcription, offering flexibility for meetings not scheduled through your calendar.

#### Managing Meeting Settings

To optimize your transcription experience, customize your meeting settings in Otter AI. You can manage recording options, such as pausing, resuming, or stopping the recording as needed. For a live transcript, ensure you have a stable internet connection.

Here’s a quick code snippet to illustrate how you might programmatically manage meeting settings using Otter AI's API:

```python

# Initialize the Otter AI client
```

## Importing Existing Audio or Video Files

### Supported File Formats

When importing existing audio or video files into transcription tools, it's crucial to know which file formats are supported. Most meeting transcription tools, such as Otter.ai, support common audio formats like MP3, WAV, and AAC, as well as video formats like MP4 and MOV. This flexibility ensures that users can easily upload recordings from a variety of sources without needing to convert files beforehand.

### Step-by-Step Import Process

Importing files into a transcription tool like Otter.ai is straightforward. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you through the process:

1. **Sign In**: Log into your Otter.ai account.
2. **Access Import Feature**: Navigate to the import section, typically found on the dashboard or within the settings menu.
3. **Select File**: Click on the import button and choose the audio or video file you wish to transcribe from your device.
4. **Upload**: Confirm your selection to begin the upload process. Ensure that your internet connection is stable to avoid interruptions.
5. **Transcription**: Once uploaded, Otter.ai will automatically start transcribing the file. This process might take a few minutes depending on the file size and length.

For online meetings, Otter.ai can automatically join and transcribe sessions by connecting to your Google or Microsoft calendar. You can specify which meetings—such as those on Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams—Otter Notetaker should join.

### Editing Transcriptions After Import

Once your file is transcribed, you may need to edit the transcription to correct any inaccuracies. This can be done directly within the Otter.ai interface:

- **Review**: Go through the transcription to identify any errors or sections that require clarification.
- **Edit**: Click on the text to make changes. Otter.ai provides an intuitive editor that allows you to easily modify the transcription.
- **Highlight and Comment**: Use highlighting and commenting features to emphasize important parts or add notes for future reference.
