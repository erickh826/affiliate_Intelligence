## Introduction to Keyword Research

# Introduction to Keyword Research

## What is Keyword Research?

Learning how to do keyword research with Ahrefs starts with understanding that keyword research is the process of discovering and analyzing search terms your target audience uses. It's the foundation of any successful SEO strategy, giving you a roadmap of what people are actually searching for in your industry.

Keyword research involves identifying high-potential search queries, measuring their difficulty and opportunity, and prioritizing them based on business value. Rather than guessing what keywords matter, you're making data-driven decisions about which terms to target.

### Getting Started with Ahrefs Keywords Explorer

To begin keyword research in Ahrefs, follow these steps:

1. **Enter seed terms** in Keywords Explorer—these are broad, foundational keywords related to your niche
2. **Examine Matching terms and Related terms reports** to uncover variations and long-tail keywords
3. **Use Site Explorer's Organic Keywords report** to see what your competitors currently rank for
4. **Review the Content Gap report** to identify keyword opportunities your competitors rank for but you don't

## Importance of Keyword Research in SEO

Keyword research directly impacts your SEO success because it aligns your content with user intent. Without it, you risk creating content nobody searches for or tackling keywords too competitive for your site's current authority.

Ahrefs recommends filtering keyword ideas by:

- **Search volume** – monthly search frequency
- **Keyword Difficulty (KD)** – competitiveness score
- **Traffic Potential** – estimated organic traffic if you rank #1
- **Clicks** – actual clicks from search results

Then prioritize by parent topic, search intent, and business value.

This combination of data-driven filtering and real-world validation ensures you're targeting keywords with genuine opportunity for your business.

# Using Keywords Explorer

## Entering Seed Terms

Keyword research in Ahrefs begins with **Keywords Explorer**, where you input seed terms—the foundational keywords related to your topic or business. These serve as the starting point for discovering related search opportunities.

**Steps to enter seed terms:**

1. Open Keywords Explorer from the Ahrefs dashboard
2. Type your seed term (e.g., "content marketing tools") into the search box
3. Select your target country to localize results
4. Click "Explore" to generate keyword data

Ahrefs processes millions of keywords across Google Search, YouTube, and Bing, providing comprehensive insights. The platform's database includes search volume, keyword difficulty, and traffic potential metrics—essential for informed prioritization.

## Understanding Matching Terms and Related Terms Reports

Once you've entered a seed term, two critical reports emerge:

**Matching Terms** displays keywords containing your exact seed phrase, sorted by search volume and keyword difficulty. This report helps identify direct variations and long-tail opportunities within your primary topic cluster.

**Related Terms** reveals semantically similar keywords that may not contain your seed phrase but share search intent. These reports often uncover high-potential keywords your competitors rank for but you haven't targeted yet.

### Filtering and Prioritization Framework

Ahrefs recommends filtering by four key metrics:

- **Search Volume**: Target keywords with sufficient monthly searches (thresholds vary by industry)
- **Keyword Difficulty (KD)**: Ranges from 0–100; lower scores indicate easier ranking opportunities
- **Traffic Potential**: Estimates organic traffic if you rank #1, accounting for SERP features
- **Clicks**: Shows actual click-through rates from Google Search results

**Critical consideration**: Don't rely solely on KD scores. Ahrefs' Keyword Difficulty is calculated from the number and authority of linking domains pointing to the top 10 ranking pages—it doesn't capture every ranking factor. Always validate by examining actual search results for your target keywords.

After filtering, prioritize keywords by:
- Parent topic (grouping related queries into content pillars)
- Search intent (informational, commercial, transactional)
- Business value (alignment with revenue and strategic goals)

## Analyzing Competitor Keywords

## Analyzing Competitor Keywords

Understanding what keywords your competitors rank for is essential to identifying gaps in your own strategy. Ahrefs provides two powerful approaches for this research.

### Using Site Explorer for Organic Keywords

Site Explorer lets you pull organic keyword data directly from competitors' rankings. Start by entering a competitor's domain, then navigate to the **Organic Keywords** report to see every keyword they rank for, along with search volume, traffic potential, and ranking position.

**Steps to analyze competitor keywords:**

1. Open Site Explorer and enter your competitor's domain
2. Click **Organic Keywords** in the left sidebar
3. Sort by **Traffic** to identify their highest-value keywords
4. Filter by **Keyword Difficulty** (KD \<30) to find easier wins
5. Export results and cross-reference against your own keyword list

- Filter by search volume and Keyword Difficulty to prioritize high-impact targets
- Sort by "Opportunities" to surface keywords where you have the best chance of ranking

# Evaluating Search Intent

## Understanding User Intent

Search intent—the reason behind a user's query—is the foundation of effective keyword research. Before targeting a keyword, you must determine whether searchers want information, wish to make a purchase, or seek a specific website. Misaligning your content with user intent wastes traffic and damages conversion rates.

Ahrefs' Keywords Explorer helps you assess intent by displaying actual search results for your seed terms. Rather than guessing intent from a keyword alone, examine the top-ranking pages to see what format and content style Google rewards. If you're targeting a commercial keyword but the top 10 results show only blog posts, that's a signal to reconsider your approach.

## Best Practices for Keyword Research

# Best Practices for Keyword Research

Effective keyword research requires moving beyond vanity metrics and validating opportunities against real-world search behavior. Here's how to refine your approach using data-driven tools and tactics.

## Checking Actual Search Results

A common mistake is trusting Keyword Difficulty (KD) scores in isolation. Ahrefs' KD metric is calculated based on the number of linking domains pointing to the top 10 ranking pages—but this captures only one ranking factor. Search intent, content quality, user experience signals, and topical authority also influence rankings.

**Always verify keywords by examining actual search results.** Here's a practical workflow:

1. Enter your seed term in Ahrefs' Keywords Explorer
2. Review the Matching Terms and Related Terms reports to expand your list
3. Cross-reference competitor rankings using Site Explorer's Organic Keywords report
4. Open the top 10 results in your browser and assess content depth, format, and freshness
5. Compare against your content capabilities before committing resources

This manual verification step reveals whether a low-KD keyword is genuinely easier to rank for or simply less competitive due to low search intent.

<AffiliateCTA partner="ahrefs" />

## Balancing Search Volume and Traffic Potential

Raw search volume is misleading—a keyword with 5,000 monthly searches might drive fewer clicks than a 2,000-volume keyword with higher click-through rates or lower competition.

Ahrefs addresses this by offering a Traffic Potential metric alongside search volume, KD, and clicks data. This gives you visibility into actual traffic upside, not just search demand. Ahrefs Starter is $29/month. Standard is $249/month. Paid plans include Keywords Explorer.

Use this filtering and prioritization framework:

- **Filter by search volume** \(aim for 100\+ monthly searches, adjusted by your market size\)
- **Check Traffic Potential** to identify keywords with outsized click volume
- **Sort by Keyword Difficulty** relative to your domain authority
- **Prioritize by parent topic** to cluster keywords into pillar content
- **Validate search intent**—match keyword intent to your content format and business goal
- **Score by business value**—rank keywords by conversion likelihood, not just traffic

Keywords with moderate search volume but high traffic potential and low competition often deliver better ROI than high-volume keywords with saturated competition.
