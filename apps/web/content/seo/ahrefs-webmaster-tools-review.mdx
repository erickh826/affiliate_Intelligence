# Introduction to Ahrefs Webmaster Tools

## What is Ahrefs Free?

This ahrefs webmaster tools review covers the rebranded free tier that Ahrefs now calls Ahrefs Free. Originally known as Ahrefs Webmaster Tools, this product has been streamlined under a single account system rather than as a separate offering. According to Ahrefs' official documentation, users who previously held Webmaster Tools accounts now access the same functionality through Ahrefs Free, which bundles multiple capabilities into one platform at no cost.

Ahrefs Free costs **$0, requires no credit card, and has no expiration date**—making it a genuine perpetual free option for website owners and SEO professionals who need basic auditing and analytics without financial commitment.

## Key Features Overview

Ahrefs Free includes six core tools: Site Explorer, Site Audit, Web Analytics, Social Media Manager, AI Content Helper, and SEO Toolbar. The platform's published limits are designed for small-to-medium projects:

- **Site Audit**: 5,000 crawl credits per verified project per month
- **Site Explorer**: up to 1,000 backlinks and keywords visible at once
- **Web Analytics**: up to 1 million events per project per month

These limits suit freelancers, small agencies, and content creators testing SEO workflows before upgrading to paid plans.

# Detailed Features of Ahrefs Free

Ahrefs Free is the rebranded version of Ahrefs Webmaster Tools, offering a genuine zero-cost entry point to professional SEO tooling. According to Ahrefs, these are the same account—not separate products—meaning you upgrade features without losing data or starting over.

## Site Explorer Capabilities

Site Explorer lets you inspect backlink profiles, keyword rankings, and organic traffic patterns without spending a dime. The free tier surfaces up to 1,000 backlinks and keywords visible at once per search, which is sufficient for competitive analysis on small to mid-sized projects. You can identify referring domains, anchor text distribution, and traffic-driving pages for any public website. This mirrors features found in paid [SEO tools]({{LINK_SEO_TOOLS}}) but with stricter data limits.

## Site Audit Functionality

The Site Audit tool crawls your website and flags technical SEO issues: broken links, duplicate content, missing meta tags, crawlability problems, and page speed concerns. Ahrefs Free allocates **5,000 Site Audit crawl credits per verified project per month**—enough for monthly full-site crawls on websites under ~2,000 pages. Each crawl consumes credits based on page count, so a 500-page site uses roughly 500 credits. Larger sites may require spreading audits across two months or upgrading to a paid plan.

## Web Analytics Insights

Web Analytics tracks visitor behavior, traffic sources, and conversion funnels directly on your domain. The free version caps data collection at **up to 1 million Web Analytics events per project per month**. For context, a modest e-commerce store generating 50,000 monthly sessions typically logs 200,000–500,000 events (depending on interaction tracking depth), leaving room for seasonal growth before hitting the ceiling.

## Ahrefs Free vs Google Search Console

Google Search Console remains the industry standard for indexation and click-through data because Google owns the search results. It's free, integrates directly with Google Search, and provides authentic impression counts and click metrics that no third-party tool can replicate.

However, Google Search Console has significant blind spots. It shows limited keyword data (typically 90 days), offers minimal backlink visibility, and provides no competitor analysis. **Ahrefs Free** ($0, no credit card required, no expiration) fills these gaps by displaying up to 1,000 backlinks and keywords visible at once in Site Explorer. Ahrefs Free also includes 5,000 Site Audit crawl credits per verified project per month—enough to audit most small-to-medium sites monthly—plus access to Web Analytics (up to 1 million events per project) and the AI Content Helper.

For SEO professionals, the combination works best: use Google Search Console for authentic performance data and Google-specific issues, then layer Ahrefs Free for backlink discovery, site health monitoring, and competitive research.

## Ahrefs Free vs Bing Webmaster Tools

Bing Webmaster Tools is free and offers indexation insights specific to Bing's crawler. For sites with significant Bing traffic, it's a necessary complement to Google Search Console. Bing also provides keyword research data that Google Console withholds.

The trade-off: Bing represents only 3–5% of global search volume in most markets, so insights apply to a smaller audience. Ahrefs Free provides broader utility because it works across search engines and includes Site Audit, backlink analysis, and content tools in a single platform.
