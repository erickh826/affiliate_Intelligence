## Getting Started with Keyword Research

# Getting Started with Keyword Research

Surfer SEO's keyword research workflow is designed to streamline the process from discovery to content creation. The platform combines clustering, filtering, and competitive analysis into a cohesive system that feeds directly into content optimization.

## Entering Your Target Keyword and Location

Begin by opening Surfer's Keyword Research module and entering your primary keyword along with your target geographic location. This foundation determines which search results Surfer analyzes and which competitors appear in your reports.

**Steps to set up your keyword research:**

1. Navigate to **Keyword Research** in your Surfer dashboard
2. Enter your target keyword in the search field
3. Select your location (country, region, or city depending on your business scope)
4. Choose your language preference
5. Specify desktop or mobile crawler type
6. Click "Analyze" to generate initial data

Surfer processes this input against real SERP data, pulling live ranking data and competitor information. The platform supports up to 20 keywords per Content Editor project, though Surfer's documentation recommends focusing on one main keyword per editor session for optimal analysis depth.

## Reviewing Keyword Clusters and Filters

After your initial analysis, Surfer organizes related search terms into semantic clusters. This clustering reveals keyword variations that search intent grouping—rather than raw similarity—connects to your primary term.

Within the clusters view, apply filters by:

- **Search Intent** (informational, commercial, transactional, navigational)
- **Search Volume** (monthly searches)
- **Keyword Difficulty** (relative ranking challenge)
- **Competition Level** (advertiser density)

Once you've identified high-potential keywords, select a cluster and send it directly to [Content Editor]({{LINK_CONTENT_EDITOR}}) to begin optimization. This eliminates manual data transfer and ensures your keyword strategy aligns with your content output.

## Conducting a Content Audit

# Conducting a Content Audit

A content audit reveals which pages underperform and where optimization effort should focus. Surfer's audit workflow combines Google Search Console data with SERP analysis to pinpoint re-optimization opportunities.

## Connecting Google Search Console

Before running an audit, you must connect your Google Search Console property to Surfer. This integration lets Surfer access your real search performance data—clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position for each page. Without this connection, Surfer cannot identify which of your existing pages rank for specific keywords or which ones have declined in visibility.

The connection process is straightforward:
1. Navigate to Surfer's Content Audit section
2. Authenticate with your Google account
3. Select the GSC property you want to analyze
4. Grant Surfer permission to read search performance data
5. Choose a date range (typically the last 90 days for trend analysis)

Once connected, Surfer pulls your search data and cross-references it with live SERP analysis to score each page's re-optimization potential.

<AffiliateCTA partner="surfer" />

## Identifying Pages for Re-optimization

Surfer's Content Audit ranks your pages by re-optimization score, showing which ones have the highest ROI potential. Pages ranking in positions 6–20 are prime candidates—they have existing authority but lack visibility to reach the top three spots.

For each page, the audit displays:
- **Current ranking position** and click volume
- **SERP competitors** and their content strategy
- **Optimization gap** (how far your content lags competitor benchmarks)
- **Keyword opportunity** (related keywords your page could target)

When you identify a page worth improving, import it directly into [Surfer's Content Editor]({{LINK_CONTENT_OPTIMIZATION}}). The editor lets you add up to 20 keywords per audit and will generate a re-optimization draft or provide manual guidance.

**Pricing note:** Surfer's audit feature is included in all paid plans, starting at $89/month for the Basic plan, which covers one project and up to 5 Content Editors monthly.

The workflow prioritizes impact: focus on pages with existing traffic that can climb higher with targeted content adjustments, rather than optimizing from zero. This approach typically yields measurable ranking and click improvements within 4–6 weeks.
