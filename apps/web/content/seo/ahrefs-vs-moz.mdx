# Ahrefs vs Moz: A Comprehensive Comparison of SEO Tools

## Overview of Ahrefs and Moz

# Overview of Ahrefs and Moz

When evaluating SEO tools, the **Ahrefs vs Moz** debate frequently surfaces among marketers and agencies. Both platforms deliver competitive intelligence, backlink analysis, and keyword research capabilities, yet they differ significantly in pricing structure, feature depth, and user experience. Understanding these differences helps teams select the tool that best aligns with their budget and workflow requirements.

## What is Ahrefs?

Ahrefs is a comprehensive SEO platform built around its proprietary web crawler, which indexes billions of webpages daily. The tool excels at backlink analysis, showing detailed link profiles for any domain, competitor gap analysis, and content research. Users can identify high-performing content across the web and discover keyword opportunities with estimated search volume and click data.

Ahrefs pricing starts at **$29/month for Starter**, scaling to $129 (Lite), $249 (Standard), $449 (Advanced), and $1,499/month for Enterprise with annual commitment. Annual billing on Lite and above saves up to 17%. Starter stays on monthly billing. Extra users are $40/month on Lite (up to 2), $60/month on Standard (up to 5), $80/month on Advanced (up to 10), and $100/month on Enterprise (unlimited, from 3 included users).

## What is Moz Pro?

Moz Pro positions itself as an accessible alternative for smaller teams and freelancers. Its core strengths include rank tracking with daily update frequency, local SEO insights via Local Listings, and the Keyword Explorer tool. Moz's interface emphasizes simplicity, making it popular among users new to SEO tooling.

Moz Pro's entry-level plan costs **$49/month**, with Standard, Medium, and Large tiers at $99, $179, and $299/month respectively. Annual pricing reduces these to $79, $143, and $239/month—meaningful savings for committed subscriptions.

## Key Features of Ahrefs

# Key Features of Ahrefs

Ahrefs is a comprehensive SEO platform designed to help marketers analyze competitor strategies, discover keywords, and optimize content performance. Its three core tools—Site Explorer, Keyword Explorer, and Content Explorer—address different stages of SEO research and strategy.

## Site Explorer

Site Explorer lets you audit any domain to uncover backlink profiles, organic traffic estimates, and technical SEO issues. You can identify which pages drive the most traffic, analyze competitor link-building patterns, and discover broken backlinks to reclaim link-building opportunities. The tool also shows keyword rankings and position history, making it valuable for tracking SEO progress over time.

## Keyword Explorer

Keyword Explorer provides search volume data, keyword difficulty scores, and click-through rate (CTR) estimates for millions of keywords. This tool helps you identify low-competition keywords worth targeting and understand search intent by analyzing the top-ranking pages for each query. You can also view keyword variations and related search terms to expand your content strategy.

## Content Explorer

Content Explorer lets you search the entire web by topic or keyword to discover which articles generate the most backlinks and organic traffic. This reverse-engineering capability reveals content gaps and shows what competitors are ranking for. You can filter results by domain, publication date, and traffic metrics to find inspiration for your next pillar content or cornerstone page.

## Key Features of Moz Pro

# Key Features of Moz Pro

Moz Pro is a comprehensive SEO platform designed to help marketers and agencies streamline their search optimization workflows. Below, we break down its three core feature sets.

## Keyword Research Tools

Moz Pro's keyword research suite provides search volume data, keyword difficulty scores, and SERP analysis. The tool integrates with Moz's proprietary metrics like Domain Authority and Page Authority, allowing you to evaluate competitor strength alongside keyword opportunity. Standard tracks 300 keywords per month, Medium tracks 1,500, and Large tracks 3,000, with daily rank tracking updates showing position changes in your target markets. The $49 entry plan covers one website.

The entry-level $49/month plan includes basic keyword research access, while higher tiers unlock expanded tracking limits and historical data. When comparing to competitors like [Ahrefs]({{LINK_AHREFS}}), Moz emphasizes usability over raw data volume—useful if you prioritize interface simplicity over exhaustive backlink indexes.

## Site Audits

Moz's site audit crawler identifies technical SEO issues including crawl errors, redirect chains, duplicate content, and mobile usability problems. The tool generates prioritized recommendations based on impact, helping you address the most critical issues first. Audit reports are available across all Moz Pro tiers, though higher plans increase crawl frequency and site size limits.

## Link Building Tools

Link Research and Link Prospector help identify high-quality link opportunities. These tools surface relevant domains in your niche and provide outreach data like contact information and email addresses. You can create custom link prospect lists filtered by Domain Authority, spam score, and traffic metrics.
