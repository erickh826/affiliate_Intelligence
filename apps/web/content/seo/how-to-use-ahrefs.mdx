# How to Use Ahrefs: A Comprehensive Tutorial

## Introduction to Ahrefs

# Introduction to Ahrefs

## What is Ahrefs?

**How to use Ahrefs** effectively starts with understanding its core function: it's a comprehensive SEO toolset designed to give you competitive intelligence and actionable insights. Ahrefs aggregates data on organic keywords, backlinks, technical issues, and content performance across millions of websites. The platform powers over a million SEO professionals and marketers worldwide, offering both enterprise-grade analytics and accessible entry points for smaller teams. Whether you're conducting competitive analysis, auditing your site, or hunting for link-building opportunities, Ahrefs centralizes these workflows into one dashboard.

## Why Use Ahrefs for SEO?

Ahrefs excels because it combines breadth with depth. Rather than juggling multiple tools, you get five integrated modules that address the full SEO lifecycle. The data freshness is industry-leading—Ahrefs crawls the web continuously, ensuring metrics reflect current rankings and backlink profiles. Pricing starts at $99/month for the Lite plan, scaling to $999/month for the Advanced tier, with custom enterprise packages available. This tiered approach makes it accessible whether you're a solo consultant or managing SEO for a large organization.

## Overview of Ahrefs Tools

Ahrefs provides five primary tools:

1. **Site Explorer** – Analyzes organic keywords, top pages, and backlinks for any domain
2. **Keywords Explorer** – Finds keyword ideas and filters by Keyword Difficulty, search volume, and intent modifiers
3. **Site Audit** – Crawls your site for technical and on-page issues, categorizing them as errors, warnings, or notices
4. **Rank Tracker** – Monitors selected keywords and tracks SERP position changes over time
5. **Content Explorer** – Discovers high-performing content and identifies link-building prospects

## Getting Started with Site Explorer

# Getting Started with Site Explorer

Ahrefs' Site Explorer is your entry point for competitive analysis and organic performance tracking. This tool pulls data on a competitor's organic keywords, top-performing pages, and backlink profile—all essential for understanding what drives traffic to any domain.

## Analyzing Organic Keywords

Site Explorer reveals every keyword a domain ranks for in organic search. When you enter a competitor's domain, you'll see estimated monthly search volume, keyword difficulty scores, and current ranking positions.

**Here's how to get started:**

1. Navigate to Site Explorer and enter your competitor's domain
2. Go to the "Organic Keywords" tab
3. Sort by "Traffic" to identify high-value keywords driving the most visitors
4. Filter by keyword difficulty to find opportunities where you might rank faster
5. Export the list for your content roadmap

This data pairs well with [Keywords Explorer]({{LINK_KEYWORD_RESEARCH}}) when you're ready to validate keyword opportunities with metrics like search intent and SERP difficulty.

## Identifying Top Pages

The "Top Pages" report shows which pages on a domain attract the most organic search traffic. This is invaluable for understanding topic clusters and content formats that resonate with your audience.

Filter by traffic, backlinks, or referring domains to spot patterns. Are their pillar pages getting the most links? Are long-form guides outperforming product pages? Use these insights to structure your own content strategy.

## Reviewing Backlinks

Site Explorer's backlink analysis reveals:

- Total referring domains and backlinks
- Link quality metrics (Domain Authority, URL Rating)
- Anchor text distribution
- New vs. lost backlinks over time

Export backlink data to identify link-building prospects or spot competitor outreach targets. Look for sites linking to multiple competitors—they're ideal candidates for your outreach.

## Keyword Research with Keywords Explorer

# Keyword Research with Keywords Explorer

Ahrefs' Keywords Explorer is purpose-built for discovering high-potential search terms and filtering them by actionable metrics. Unlike generic search tools, it connects keyword data directly to traffic potential and competition levels, making it essential for content strategists.

## Finding Keyword Ideas

Start by entering a seed term—a broad topic related to your niche. Keywords Explorer returns hundreds of related keywords organized by search volume and relevance. For competitive analysis, enter a competitor's domain into Site Explorer first to identify their top-performing pages and the keywords driving traffic to them. This reveals content gaps you can target.

The tool's matching terms feature expands your seed term into variations. For example, entering "keyword research" generates phrases like "free keyword research tools," "keyword research for SEO," and "best keyword research software." You can filter these by intent modifiers (how-to, best, review, vs.) to align with your content strategy.

## Filtering by Keyword Difficulty

Keyword Difficulty (KD) scores range from 0–100, indicating how hard it is to rank for a term on the first page of Google. A KD of 20–40 typically represents achievable targets for newer sites, while scores above 60 signal highly competitive spaces.

Ahrefs pricing starts at $99/month for the Lite plan, which includes limited Keywords Explorer access. The Standard plan ($199/month) and Advanced plan ($399/month) unlock deeper filtering and higher API limits—up to 10,000 API credits monthly on Advanced.

## Using Seed Terms Effectively

Maximize Keywords Explorer by following this workflow:

1. **Enter your primary seed term** (e.g., "content marketing")
2. **Filter by KD score** to find low-competition opportunities (\<40)
3. **Check Search Volume** to ensure sufficient monthly search traffic (\>100)
4. **Review Top Pages** to analyze current ranking content
5. **Export results** in CSV format for your content calendar

Pro tip: Combine multiple seed terms to uncover long-tail keywords with lower KD scores. A 20-word phrase targeting a specific problem typically faces less competition than broad two-word terms.

## Conducting a Site Audit

# Conducting a Site Audit

Ahrefs' Site Audit tool crawls your entire website to identify technical and on-page issues that could harm search rankings. Unlike generic crawlers, it flags problems within Ahrefs' own ranking data context, making findings immediately actionable.

## Creating a Project

Start by setting up a new project in Ahrefs. You have two import options:

1. **Import from Google Search Console** – connects directly to your GSC account and auto-populates your site's URL
2. **Add manually** – enter your domain and configure crawl settings (crawl depth, request rate, user agent)

Ahrefs pricing starts at $99/month for the Lite plan, which includes one active site audit project. Pro and Business tiers ($399/month and $999/month respectively) allow multiple concurrent projects.

<AffiliateCTA partner="ahrefs" />

## Running a Crawl

Once your project is created, initiate a crawl:

```
1. Click "Start a new crawl"
2. Select crawl depth (full site, directory-specific, or URL list)
3. Set crawl rate (default: 10 requests/second)
4. Review robots.txt and sitemap settings
5. Execute crawl (duration varies by site size)
```

A typical enterprise site (\<100K pages) completes in 2–4 hours. Ahrefs crawls up to 100,000 URLs per crawl on all plans.

## Understanding Errors, Warnings, and Notices

After crawling completes, the **Overview** tab displays your issue breakdown. Ahrefs categorizes findings into three severity levels:

- **Errors** – critical issues (broken links returning 404s, server errors, redirect chains) that directly impact indexing or user experience
- **Warnings** – potential problems (missing title tags, duplicate content, slow page speed) that may affect rankings
- **Notices** – informational items (pages without meta descriptions, underscore characters in URLs) that are less urgent but worth reviewing

Navigate to **All Issues** to filter by issue type, URL pattern, or severity. The **Top Issues** section prioritizes fixes by estimated impact on your site's crawlability and SEO.

For keyword research alongside auditing, use Ahrefs' Keywords Explorer to identify high-value terms attracting search traffic to competitor sites. Then cross-reference crawl results to ensure your target pages are technically optimized to rank for those keywords.

## Tracking Your Rankings with Rank Tracker

# Tracking Your Rankings with Rank Tracker

Ahrefs' Rank Tracker is a dedicated tool within the Ahrefs suite that monitors your selected keywords over time, showing you exactly how your pages rank in search results. Unlike one-time keyword research, this feature provides continuous visibility into your SEO performance across multiple search engines and locations.

## Setting Up Rank Tracker

Begin by creating a new project in Rank Tracker:

1. Log into your Ahrefs account (plans start at $99/month for the Lite tier)
2. Navigate to Rank Tracker and select "Add project"
3. Enter your target domain and select your primary search engine and location
4. Add keywords manually or import them from a CSV file
5. Run your first crawl to establish baseline rankings

The tool will scan search results for your keywords and record your current position. Unlike free rank trackers, Ahrefs updates rankings daily and provides historical data stretching back months, allowing you to spot trends rather than react to daily fluctuations.

## Monitoring Selected Keywords

Once your keywords are tracked, Rank Tracker displays them in an intuitive dashboard showing:

- **Current rank** — Your position in search results (1–100+)
- **Rank change** — Movement compared to previous tracking period
- **Search volume** — Monthly searches for each term
- **Keyword difficulty** — Competitive landscape metrics

You can segment keywords by status (ranking, gaining, losing) and set up email notifications when rankings change significantly. This integration with other Ahrefs tools—like [Site Explorer]({{LINK_SITE_EXPLORER}}) and Keywords Explorer—lets you correlate ranking shifts with on-page changes or competitor activity.

## Comparing Competitors' Performance

Rank Tracker's competitive intelligence features reveal which keywords your competitors rank for but you don't. To compare performance:

1. Add competitor domains to your Rank Tracker project
2. Review the "Competitors" section to see overlapping and unique rankings
3. Export keyword lists showing competitor positions versus yours
4. Identify ranking gaps where competitors outrank you by 2–5 positions

This data directly informs your content strategy. If a competitor ranks #3 for a high-value keyword while you're unranked, you've identified a concrete optimization opportunity. Ahrefs' Lite plan tracks up to 5,000 keywords per project, while higher tiers support unlimited tracking—essential for larger SEO campaigns.

Rank Tracker transforms raw ranking data into actionable insights, turning guesswork into measurable progress toward your organic search goals.

## Exploring Content Opportunities with Content Explorer

# Exploring Content Opportunities with Content Explorer

Ahrefs' Content Explorer is a powerful tool for discovering what content resonates with audiences and identifying link-building opportunities at scale. Whether you're researching competitor strategies or hunting for topics with proven traffic potential, this tool streamlines the discovery process.

## Finding Content Ideas

Content Explorer lets you search for articles and pages across the web by topic, domain, or keyword. Start by entering a broad topic related to your niche—say "sustainable packaging"—and the tool returns thousands of existing pieces ranked by estimated search traffic and social shares.

The workflow is straightforward:

1. Navigate to Content Explorer
2. Enter your topic or keyword
3. Filter results by minimum traffic, domain rating, or publication date
4. Sort by "Organic Traffic" to surface high-performing pieces
5. Click into top results to analyze their backlink profiles and topic angles

This reveals content gaps: if competitors rank articles on "sustainable packaging for ecommerce" but nothing covers "sustainable packaging for food startups," you've found your angle.

## Link-Building Prospects

Content Explorer doubles as a link prospecting tool. After identifying target content, examine its backlink profile to find websites already linking to similar topics. These sites represent warm outreach opportunities—they've already demonstrated interest in your subject matter.

Filter by domain authority and relevance to prioritize high-quality prospects. The tool shows which pages link to each piece, their anchor text, and the linking domain's metrics, enabling precise outreach campaigns.

## Filtering for High Traffic Topics

Ahrefs offers multiple subscription tiers starting at **$99/month** for the Lite plan, scaling to $999/month for Agency. Content Explorer is available on all paid plans. Each tier includes different API limits and feature access.

To maximize Content Explorer:

- **Filter by traffic volume**: Set minimum organic traffic thresholds to focus on topics with proven audience demand
- **Date range filtering**: Identify recent trending topics or evergreen content strategies
- **Domain metrics**: Screen by Domain Rating (DR) or Ahrefs Rank (AR) to benchmark against competitors
- **Search intent**: Combine filters to find content matching your target audience's stage in the buyer journey

For example, searching "email automation" filtered for content with \>10,000 monthly organic traffic and DR \>40 surfaces authoritative, high-traffic targets worth reverse-engineering or improving upon.

Content Explorer transforms guesswork into data-driven strategy, revealing where audience attention concentrates and which publishers control relevant link-building channels.

## Best Practices for Using Ahrefs

# Best Practices for Using Ahrefs

## Regularly Monitor Your Site

Ahrefs offers a robust **Site Audit** tool that crawls your entire website to identify technical and on-page issues before they harm rankings. The audit categorizes findings into three severity levels: errors, warnings, and notices. This systematic approach lets you prioritize fixes by impact.

To start a technical audit, follow these steps:

1. Create a new project in Ahrefs
2. Import your site from Google Search Console or add it manually
3. Run a full crawl of your domain
4. Review the **Overview** dashboard for critical metrics
5. Navigate to **All issues** and filter by severity or issue type
6. Address errors first, then warnings, then notices

Ahrefs pricing starts at $99/month for the Lite plan, which includes up to 5 projects and 5,000 credits per month—sufficient for small sites and freelancers running periodic audits.

<AffiliateCTA partner="ahrefs" />

## Stay Updated with Competitor Analysis

Understanding what your competitors rank for is essential for identifying content gaps. Use **Site Explorer** to analyze any competitor's domain and uncover their top-performing pages by organic search traffic. This reveals which topics are generating results in your niche.

The recommended workflow is straightforward:

- Enter a competitor's domain into Site Explorer
- Click **Top pages** to see pages ranked for organic keywords
- Review estimated monthly search traffic per page
- Note topic clusters and content formats that attract traffic
- Identify underserved angles you could target

For keyword research depth, **Keywords Explorer** filters seed terms by actionable metrics. Enter a base term, open **Matching terms**, and filter by modifiers like "how," "review," or "best" to find intent-rich variations with lower competition.

## Utilize All Ahrefs Tools Effectively

Each Ahrefs module serves a distinct purpose. **Rank Tracker** monitors your selected keywords across search engines, showing daily ranking fluctuations and traffic estimates. **Content Explorer** identifies trending content and link-building prospects by analyzing millions of published articles.

The synergy between these tools matters: use Keywords Explorer to find opportunities, Content Explorer to see what's already ranking, Site Audit to fix technical issues, and Rank Tracker to measure improvement over time.

Even at the entry level, Ahrefs provides sufficient data to make informed SEO decisions. The platform's interface rewards consistent users who regularly check Site Audit reports, review competitor Site Explorer snapshots, and track keyword movements in Rank Tracker. Success comes from systematic monitoring, not one-time analysis.
