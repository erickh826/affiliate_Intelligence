## Overview of Ahrefs in 2026

# Overview of Ahrefs in 2026

## What is Ahrefs?

This ahrefs review 2026 covers one of the most widely adopted SEO platforms available to marketers, agencies, and enterprises. Ahrefs is a comprehensive SEO toolkit designed to help users research competitors, analyze backlinks, track keyword rankings, and audit websites for technical SEO issues. The platform combines a massive web crawler with detailed analytics to provide actionable insights for search engine optimization and content strategy.

Since its launch, Ahrefs has grown into a foundational tool for SEO professionals who need reliable data on link profiles, organic search traffic, and competitor strategies. The platform serves users ranging from freelance SEO consultants to Fortune 500 companies, making it a central player in the competitive SEO software landscape.

## Key Features and Tools

Ahrefs offers five paid subscription tiers to accommodate different user needs and budgets:

- **Starter**: $29/month
- **Lite**: $129/month (includes 1,000 report credits per user monthly)
- **Standard**: $249/month (unlimited report credits)
- **Advanced**: $449/month (unlimited report credits)
- **Enterprise**: from $1,499/month with annual commitment

The Lite plan and above provide access to core features including Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Rank Tracker, and Content Gap analysis. Report credits allow users to generate customized reports; Lite-tier subscribers receive 1,000 credits per user per month, while Standard and Advanced plans offer unlimited credits. Additional users and premium add-ons incur extra costs.

## Ahrefs vs. Moz Pro

Moz Pro positions itself as the accessible alternative, targeting agencies and freelancers with simpler workflows. Moz's domain authority metrics remain industry-standard for link evaluation, though Ahrefs' Domain Rating has gained traction for its transparency in methodology.

Ahrefs outpaces Moz Pro in raw data volume—particularly for international markets and historical trend tracking. Moz excels in user experience simplicity and community resources like their Whiteboard Friday content series. Budget-conscious teams often choose Moz, while enterprises building sophisticated SEO operations select Ahrefs for its API capabilities and deeper competitive intelligence.
