# Overview of Frase and Surfer SEO

## What is Frase?

Frase is a content optimization and research platform that combines AI-powered content briefs with SEO analysis. When evaluating **frase vs surfer seo**, it's important to understand that Frase positions itself primarily as a content brief generator and research tool. It ingests top-ranking search results and synthesizes them into actionable outlines, helping writers understand what topics and subtopics dominate search intent. Frase also includes SERP analysis, competitor content audits, and AI writing assistance for scaling content production.

## What is Surfer SEO?

Surfer SEO specializes in on-page optimization and content performance analytics. Unlike Frase's focus on pre-writing research, Surfer emphasizes real-time SERP data, content scoring against top-ranking pages, and keyword density recommendations. Surfer also offers a content editor that flags optimization opportunities as you write, making it a workflow-integrated tool for teams already committed to the Surfer ecosystem.
