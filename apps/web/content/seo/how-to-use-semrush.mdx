## Keyword Magic Tool: Finding the Right Keywords

The Keyword Magic Tool is Semrush's core discovery engine. It generates keyword variations and related terms from a seed phrase, displaying search volume, keyword difficulty (KD), and cost-per-click (CPC) data.

**Basic workflow:**

1. Enter your seed keyword (e.g., "content marketing software")
2. Filter by search volume, difficulty, and intent (informational, navigational, transactional, commercial)
3. Export results to CSV or save to lists for campaign planning
4. Cross-reference with competitor keywords using the Keyword Gap tool

The tool accesses over 25 billion keywords and updates monthly. For detailed filtering, use the API if integrating with your workflow. All plans above the free tier include access; free accounts get limited results (100 keywords max per report).

## Site Audit: Analyzing Your Website's Health

Site Audit crawls your entire domain to identify technical SEO issues—broken links, missing meta tags, duplicate content, and mobile usability problems. The tool generates a health score (0–100) and prioritizes fixes by impact.

**Running an audit:**

1. Add your domain and set crawl preferences (crawl budget, user-agent, authentication)
2. Wait for completion (typically 10–30 minutes depending on site size)
3. Review the dashboard for critical, warning, and notice-level issues
4. Export detailed reports for developer handoff

Guru and Business plans allow unlimited audits; Pro includes two per month.

## Position Tracking: Monitoring Your Rankings

Position Tracking monitors your target keywords' ranking positions daily across Google, Bing, and Yandex. It surfaces ranking changes, competitor movement, and visibility trends over time.

Set up tracking by importing your keyword list, selecting search locations and devices (desktop/mobile), and choosing check frequency. The tool sends alerts when rankings drop significantly or competitors rank above you. Historical data spans up to two years, enabling trend analysis.

All paid plans include position tracking. Free accounts cannot access this feature. Additional users on any plan that includes the SEO Toolkit start at $45/month, scaling for growing teams.

These three tools form the backbone of technical SEO work in Semrush, from discovery to audit to ongoing monitoring.

## Setting Up Projects for Better Insights

Start by creating separate projects for each domain you manage. This segmentation prevents data bleed and gives you domain-specific tracking.

**Follow these steps:**

1. Log into your Semrush account and navigate to **Projects** in the left sidebar
2. Click **Create Project** and select your primary domain
3. Configure competitors (add 3–5 direct competitors for meaningful benchmarking)
4. Set tracking keywords—start with 50–100 high-intent keywords relevant to your business
5. Enable weekly automated reports to track progress without manual checks

If you're on the free plan, you get one demo project and 10 reports per day across core tools—enough to test the platform, but upgrade to Pro ($139.95/month) or higher for unlimited reporting and multiple projects.
