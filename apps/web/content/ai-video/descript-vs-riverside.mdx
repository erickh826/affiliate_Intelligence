### What is Descript?

Descript is a versatile audio and video editing tool that simplifies the editing process by allowing users to edit audio by editing text. It is particularly popular among podcasters and video creators for its transcription capabilities and ease of use. Descript offers features like multi-track editing, screen recording, and overdub, which allows users to create synthetic voiceovers. The tool is designed to streamline the editing process, making it accessible even to those with minimal technical skills.

### What is Riverside?

Riverside is a remote recording tool that focuses on providing high-quality audio and video recording capabilities. It is particularly suited for podcasters who need to record interviews remotely while maintaining studio-level quality. Riverside records locally on each participant's device and uploads the files to the cloud, ensuring that internet issues do not affect the recording quality. This makes it an ideal choice for creators who prioritize high fidelity in their productions.

**Riverside Features:**
