## What are AI Video Generators?

AI video generators are software platforms that use machine learning and generative AI to automate video creation, editing, and enhancement. These tools range from text-to-video converters to intelligent editing assistants, enabling creators to produce professional-quality content without extensive technical skills. When evaluating the **best AI video generators 2026**, you'll find solutions targeting everything from social media snippets to full-length marketing campaigns. Many platforms offer browser-based interfaces, API access, and integration with popular design tools, making them accessible to solo creators and enterprise teams alike.
