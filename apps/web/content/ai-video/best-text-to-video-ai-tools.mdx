# Introduction to Text to Video AI Tools

## What are Text to Video AI Tools?

Text to video AI tools are software platforms that convert written prompts or scripts into video content using artificial intelligence and machine learning. These best text to video ai tools leverage neural networks trained on vast video datasets to generate realistic footage, animations, transitions, and effects based solely on text descriptions. Users can input detailed prompts—specifying scenes, characters, camera movements, music, and visual styles—and the AI renders complete video sequences in minutes rather than hours or days of manual production.

The technology powers everything from marketing clips and social media content to educational videos and product demonstrations. Unlike traditional video creation, which requires cameras, editing software, and production expertise, text to video platforms democratize content creation by removing technical barriers.

Runway offers flexible pricing tiers starting at $0 for a free plan with limited monthly credits. Pika uses a credit-based system rather than fixed monthly tiers. Free users receive 250 monthly credits; one 6-second video generation typically costs 10 credits. ## Monthly Subscription Costs

Most text-to-video platforms employ tiered pricing models targeting individual creators, small teams, and enterprises. ## Free Trials and Discounts

Most tools offer free trials or freemium access to reduce adoption friction. Free tiers typically include limited monthly credits (50–500 depending on the platform), lower output resolutions, or restricted video lengths. Annual billing discounts range from 15% to 30% off monthly rates.

Runway ML and Pika frequently run seasonal promotions targeting education and nonprofit organizations, often providing 30–50% discounts. Bundled subscriptions—pairing video tools with related services like image generation or editing—can offer 10–20% savings compared to standalone pricing.

Comparison shopping is essential because cost-per-minute varies dramatically. A platform charging $20/month with 5 minutes included costs $4 per minute; one charging $30/month with 20 minutes costs $1.50 per minute. Budget-conscious teams should calculate total cost of ownership based on their monthly output requirements rather than relying on headline pricing.
