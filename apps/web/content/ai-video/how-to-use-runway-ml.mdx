## Creating Image-to-Video Clips

### Creating Image-to-Video Clips

In this section, we'll explore how to generate video clips from images using Runway's Gen-4 models, understand the differences between Gen-4 and Gen-4 Turbo, and provide tips for crafting effective motion prompts.

#### Step-by-Step Guide to Generate Video

Creating an image-to-video clip with Runway's Gen-4 models is a straightforward process. Follow these steps:

1. **Access the Dashboard**: Log in to your Runway account and navigate to the dashboard.
2. **Select Generate Video**: Choose the 'Generate Video' option.
3. **Choose Your Model**: Decide between Gen-4 and Gen-4 Turbo based on your needs.
4. **Upload an Image**: Select the image you wish to transform into a video.
5. **Enter a Motion Prompt**: Input a descriptive prompt that dictates the motion or transformation you want to see in the video.
6. **Set Duration**: Choose either a 5-second or 10-second video duration.
7. **Generate and Download**: Click 'Generate' to create your video. Once completed, download it or find it in the 'Assets' section.

Both Gen-4 models require an input image to start the process. For more detailed guidance, refer to Runway's [help article](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/37327109429011-Creating-with-Gen-4-Video).

#### Choosing the Right Model: Gen-4 vs Gen-4 Turbo

When deciding between Gen-4 and Gen-4 Turbo, consider the following:

- **Gen-4**: Ideal for general use with balanced performance and quality.
- **Gen-4 Turbo**: Offers faster processing times, which can be beneficial for time-sensitive projects or when working with larger batches of videos.

Pricing for these models starts at $15 per month for the basic plan, which provides access to both Gen-4 and Gen-4 Turbo features, along with other tools. For more detailed pricing information, visit [Runway's pricing page](https://runway.com/pricing).

#### Tips for Effective Motion Prompts

Creating compelling motion prompts is crucial for achieving the desired video output. Here are some tips:

- **Be Descriptive**: Clearly describe the visual elements and their movements. For example, "a gentle breeze causing leaves to sway" provides a clear motion directive.
- **Consider Scene Dynamics**: Think about how different elements interact within the scene. This can enhance the realism and depth of the video.
- **Experiment with Variations**: Try different prompts to see which yields the best results. Subtle changes can significantly impact the final video.

For a deeper dive into crafting effective prompts, check out Runway's [Academy guide](https://academy.runwayml.com/guides/prompting-guide).

By following these guidelines and utilizing Runway's Gen-4 models, you can create stunning image-to-video clips efficiently and effectively.

