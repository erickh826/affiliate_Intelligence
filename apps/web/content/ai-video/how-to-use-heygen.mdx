HeyGen offers a straightforward process for creating videos:

1. **Sign In**: Access your HeyGen account.
2. **Create Video**: Select 'Create Video' and choose 'Start from Scratch.'
3. **Orientation**: Pick the desired video orientation.
4. **Add Script**: Input your script for the video.
5. **Select Avatar and Voice**: Choose from a variety of avatars and voices.
6. **Preview**: Review your project to ensure everything is as desired.
7. **Generate**: Click 'Generate' to create the video, which will then appear in your Projects section.

```plaintext
  voice: 'english_female'

  console.log('Video created:', video.url);
```

## Getting Started with HeyGen

### Getting Started with HeyGen

HeyGen is a powerful tool for creating AI-avatar videos, offering a user-friendly interface and a variety of features to enhance your video projects. Here’s how you can get started with HeyGen, from setting up your account to navigating the dashboard.

#### Creating an Account

To begin using HeyGen, you first need to create an account. Visit the HeyGen website and click on the 'Sign Up' button. You'll be prompted to enter your email address and create a password. Once you've completed these steps, you'll receive a confirmation email to verify your account.

**Pricing:** HeyGen offers a tiered pricing model. The basic plan starts at $30 per month, which includes access to standard features and a limited number of video exports. For more advanced features and higher export limits, you can opt for the Pro plan at $60 per month.

#### Navigating the Dashboard

Once your account is set up, you can log in to access the HeyGen dashboard. The dashboard is your central hub for creating and managing video projects. Here's a quick guide to help you navigate:

1. **Create a New Video**: 
   - Sign in to your account.
   - Select 'Create Video' and choose 'Start from Scratch'.
   - Choose the video orientation (landscape, portrait, or square).

2. **Add Content**: 
   - Enter your script in the provided text box.
   - Select an avatar and voice from the available options.

3. **Preview and Generate**: 
   - Preview your project to ensure everything looks and sounds as expected.
   - Click 'Generate' to create your video. The finished video will appear in the 'Projects' section.

4. **Manage Projects**: 
   - Access your projects from the dashboard to edit, download, or share them as needed.

Here is a simple code snippet to illustrate how you might interact with HeyGen's API to automate video creation:

```python
import heygen_api

client = heygen_api.Client(api_key='YOUR_API_KEY')

    avatar="default_avatar",
```

## Step-by-Step Guide to Creating a Video

### Step-by-Step Guide to Creating a Video

Creating a video using AI tools like HeyGen and its alternatives can streamline your content production process. This guide will walk you through the steps of creating a video, from signing in to adding your script. Note that the tools mentioned, including HeyGen, are not ranked by market share. 

#### Signing In and Starting a New Project

To begin creating a video with HeyGen, you'll need to sign in to your account. If you don't have an account yet, you'll need to create one. Once signed in, follow these steps:

1. **Select 'Create Video'**: From the dashboard, click on 'Create Video.'
2. **Choose 'Start from Scratch'**: This option allows you to build your video project from the ground up.

HeyGen offers a free trial, but for extended features, pricing starts at $30 per month. This includes access to a variety of avatars and voices.

#### Choosing Video Orientation

The next step is to select the orientation of your video. HeyGen provides several options to suit different platforms, such as:

- **Landscape**: Ideal for YouTube and similar platforms.
- **Portrait**: Best for mobile-first platforms like Instagram and TikTok.
- **Square**: A versatile option for social media.

Choosing the right orientation is crucial as it affects how your audience will engage with your content.

#### Adding Your Script

Once you've set up your project and chosen an orientation, it's time to add your script. Here's how you can do it:

1. **Add a Script**: Click on the script section and type or paste your text.
2. **Select an Avatar and Voice**: Choose from a range of AI-generated avatars and voices to bring your script to life.
3. **Preview the Project**: Before finalizing, preview your video to ensure everything looks and sounds as expected.

```python
# Example code to add a script in HeyGen

project.set_orientation('landscape')
project.add_script("Welcome to our product demo!")
project.select_avatar('Professional')
project.select_voice('English_Female')
project.preview()
```

4. **Generate the Video**: Once satisfied, click 'Generate.' The finished video will appear in your Projects section.
