HeyGen's avatar generation is a standout feature, offering users the ability to create realistic avatars with varying levels of detail and animation. The platform provides two main types of avatars: Avatar V and Avatar IV.

One of the user-friendly aspects of HeyGen's credit system is its rollover policy. For annual subscribers, unused credits accumulate until the annual renewal date. However, it's important to note that unused credits do not carry over if a paid account is downgraded to a Free plan. This policy ensures that subscribers can maximize their investment in HeyGen's services without the pressure of losing unused credits each month. More details can be found in HeyGen's [help article](https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/15126059-how-to-use-credits-on-heygen).
